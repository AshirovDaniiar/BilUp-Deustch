## Prerequisite knowledge (checked against the A1 data in this repository)

* A1 unit 8 `perfekt`: Perfekt with haben/sein, regular and irregular Partizip II, separable prefix, "motion → sein". So unit 11 *deepens* Perfekt; it does not introduce it.
* A1 unit 6 `conjunctions`: **`weil` (verb at the end) and `denn` are already taught in A1** with a "weil + simple present" frame. Consequence for unit 11: concept 3's `weil` is revision plus a new load (Perfekt/modal at the end of the clause); the genuinely new parts of concept 3 are the sequence adverbs and `dass`.
* A1 unit 4 `modal`: modal + infinitive at the end of the clause (present tense). Unit 11 adds the Präteritum forms.
* Not taught before unit 11: Präteritum of any verb, `dass`, `seit`/`vor` (time), reflexive verbs in Perfekt.
