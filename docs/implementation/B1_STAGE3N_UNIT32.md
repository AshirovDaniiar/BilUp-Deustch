## 5. Texts
Reading long **215** words (band 200–250), short **94** (80–150), listening **125** and **133** (120–220); sentences ≤ 28, p95 ≤ 22. Exam-style: the long text is a Lernbilanz, the short one a course notice stating that the Probe is "keine offizielle Prüfung"; both use the three-option format and the statements "richtig / falsch / steht nicht im Text" (all three answers occur). Listenings: a Durchsage + conversation, and a short talk by one speaker (existing browser text-to-speech lab; no audio file). Speaking: **8 turns** (Einleitung → two points → reaction to an objection → joint planning → the unexpected "room is taken" → who brings what → Abschiedsworte); checklist and self-assessment, no score. Writing: **two main tasks** — informal e-mail (min 80 words, 7 expected elements) and formal letter (min 70, 6 elements) — plus a learning-diary micro-task (`note`, min 30, 3 elements); every model text meets its own checklist.
