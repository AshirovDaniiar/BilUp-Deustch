They are tagged A2, so they were not imported; destinations stay empty. The A1 `genitiv` lesson is tagged **A2** too and has no approved A1 topic, so it stays in hidden legacy Unit 10.

**D. `wechsel` for 35.** The lesson teaches Wo? → Dativ and Wohin? → Akkusativ together (the contrast is the point). Topic 35 is the Wo? half; the Wohin? half is part of the same lesson and no other approved topic is "Wechselpräpositionen: Wohin?". Treated as a semantic match; flagged here so you can overrule.

**E. Why partial ≠ assigned.** 07/08, 19/20 and 27/28 are *pairs* that the legacy curriculum teaches in one lesson each; assigning that lesson to both units would duplicate it, assigning it to one would silently leave the other empty. 27/28 additionally do not match the approved verb list (no *wollen*, no *dürfen*). 31 would teach *weil* inside a topic that excludes subordinate clauses.
